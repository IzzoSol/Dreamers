# Dreamers Artist Showcase

A platform within the Dreamers Ecosystem where independent artists can showcase and sell their work, powered by $DREAM and Solana NFTs.

## How It Works

1. Artists apply → curated or open submission
2. Mint their work as NFTs on the platform
3. Fans buy with $DREAM → artist gets 80-90% of primary sale
4. Secondary sales have enforced royalties
5. Every transaction feeds the ecosystem tax

## Artist Benefits

- Direct-to-fan sales, no label/middleman
- Built-in audience from game players
- $DREAM token payments (convertible to SOL/USDC)
- Exposure through dashboard + game integration
- Community voting on featured artists (DAO)

## Ecosystem Benefits

- Brings new audiences (each artist's fanbase)
- More $DREAM transactions → more burns → healthier token
- Cultural depth — not just a game, it's a creative movement
- Content for social media (artist spotlights, drops, events)

## Artist Categories

- **Musicians:** Singles, albums, stems, instrumentals
- **Visual Artists:** Digital art, album covers, game sprites
- **Writers:** Poetry, stories, lyrics
- **Producers:** Beats, sound packs
- **Photographers:** Limited edition prints as NFTs

## Revenue Split

| Sale Type | Artist | Treasury | Burn | Dev |
|-----------|--------|----------|------|-----|
| Primary | 85% | 5% | 5% | 5% |
| Secondary | 4% royalty | 2% | 2% | 2% |
