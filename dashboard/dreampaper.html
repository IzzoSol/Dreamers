The Dreamers Vision — Vision & Roadmap

The Culture of Creation

Dreamers is a creator-owned platform for music, art, books, merch and wellness — a home for the people who make the culture. This page is the vision, written honestly: what is live today, and what is still just a roadmap.

How to read this page

LIVE means it exists and you can use it today. ROADMAP / PROPOSAL means an idea for the future — not a product, not a promise, not a date. And plainly, up front: no yields, no revenue projections, and nothing on this page is an investment offer.

01 — The Vision

A home for creators

Every platform takes a cut of the culture and gives the creator the leftovers. Dreamers is being built the other way: a place where an artist's music, books, merch, lessons and community live under one roof — owned by the creator, not rented from an algorithm.

It starts with one artist's real catalog — the music of Izzo, the books of Israel Hicks, the CloudzNCrownz merch line — and grows into a home any creator can move into: make in the studio, gather in the spaces, learn, sell, and keep what you earn.

The mission, in one line

Build the infrastructure for a creator-owned culture. Make the music. Own your space. Teach what you know. Create the culture — and get paid for it through real work and real sales.

02 — Live Today

What exists now

No vapor. Everything in this section is on this site or in the stores today:

  • The album, streaming here — The Good Samaritan by Izzo plays in full on the home page, alongside the Good Person music video.
  • The browser studio — music-making tools, a gallery, 3D worlds and the Dreamers Climb game, all running in the browser.
  • Books, published — The Roots of Medicine is on sale through the IzzoSol Gumroad store; eight more finished books follow as their listings publish.
  • Merch, designed — the full CloudzNCrownz / Good Samaritan catalog is designed and ready; the print-on-demand stores open next (see the roadmap).
  • Spaces & sessions — community music sessions and a shared word bank, built for creators to make songs together.
  • Wellness — breath, movement and day-building practices woven through the dashboard, drawn from the books.
03 — Music First

The music is the heartbeat

Dreamers began as a record: The Good Samaritan (2022) — ten tracks, with Locco on "No Reason" and Dano Detroit on "Sun." The writing is registered with BMI and the publishing is administered by Songtrust, the ordinary, real machinery that pays songwriters when their songs are played.

The plan for the music is simple and honest: keep the catalog live on streaming, sell the complete album-plus-features bundle directly to listeners through Gumroad, and let the songs pull people into the world — the videos, the merch, the books, the spaces. No fractional anything. A song is a song; you can listen to it, buy it, and wear it.

04 — Books, Merch & Wellness

The rest of the house

Books

Nine finished books by Israel Hicks — transformation, breath, medicine's roots, a full workout, a guided meditation, and THE PLAN trilogy — rolling out on Gumroad and Kindle. Every one carries the same note: this is what I have studied so far, I am not perfect, and the book is a stepping stone for your own digging.

Merch

The designs exist — dozens of them, printed on demand by Printful and Printify so nothing is stocked and nothing is wasted. The storefronts open as soon as the store setup is finished; until then the home page shows the catalog as a showcase and says so.

Wellness

The practices in the books — breathwork, the Infinity Hour, the written day — live inside the dashboard as daily tools. Wellness here is a practice, not a product claim: no cures promised, ever.

05 — Sparks

Sparks — the platform credit

Sparks, stated plainly

A Spark is a platform credit. 1 Spark = $1, fixed, always. Sparks pay for platform services inside Dreamers. A Spark is not on any blockchain, it is not tradeable, it is not an investment, and it will never become one.

That's the whole design. Credits exist so creators and fans can pay for things inside the platform without friction — nothing more speculative than a gift card, and labeled like one.

06 — Roadmap · Proposal

Where this could go

Everything below is a PROPOSAL — directions being explored, in the order they would be built. None of it is promised and none of it has a date:

  • Stores fully open — the Printful and Printify storefronts live, the merch catalog buyable from this site, the music bundle on sale.
  • Deeper 3D spaces — creator-owned rooms and worlds you can walk through, perform in, and decorate.
  • Education — lessons and courses from working creators, built on the books and the session format.
  • Agent tools — AI helpers inside the studio and dashboard that organize, draft and build alongside the creator.
  • More creators — the roof opens: other artists bring their catalogs into Dreamers the same way the first one did.
On Sparks — the standing rule

Sparks are the only currency inside Dreamers — a platform credit fixed at $1. The platform comes first. Anyone selling you something else under the Dreamers name is not selling you ours.

07 — Honest Print

What this page is not

This page used to be a whitepaper. Earlier editions described yields of 5–35%, burn schedules and revenue projections as if they were real. None of that was ever launched. Those were concepts written ahead of the truth, and they have been removed — because a vision page that invents numbers teaches nobody to trust the vision.

So, for the record: this page is not a whitepaper, not a yield offer, and not a projection of anyone's earnings. It is a vision and a roadmap, labeled LIVE where it is live and PROPOSAL where it is not. When something on the roadmap ships, this page will say so — with the link, so you can check.

The culture of creation is real. The catalog is real. The rest is work. Come watch it get built.
