Vision & Mission

Dreamers is a platform where creators make music, art, and worlds — and own everything they create. No gatekeepers. No middlemen. Just creators, their tools, and their audience.

Mission

Build the infrastructure for a creator-owned economy. Tokenize songs. Own digital spaces. Earn from art. Connect to AI agents. Create the culture — and get paid for it.

Why This Exists

  • Artists don't own their work. Platforms take 30-50%. Labels take more. Creators get scraps.
  • Music royalties are opaque. Artists can't see who listens, where revenue goes, or how much they earn.
  • Digital spaces are rented. You don't own your Instagram, your YouTube, your Twitch. They can delete you tomorrow.
  • REFI is inaccessible. Carbon credits and nature-based assets are locked behind institutional barriers.
  • AI is disconnected. AI agents exist in silos. They don't connect to your music, your art, your economy.

Dreamers fixes all of this. One platform. One economy. One culture.

Tokenized Songs — $CNC

$CNC (Culture Notes Credit) represents fractional ownership shares in songs. When an artist tokenizes a song, $CNC tokens are minted representing ownership. Holders earn royalties when the song is played, licensed, or used in Dreamers.

How It Works

Artist creates a song │ ├── Song minted as NFT (metadata, audio, rights) ├── 1,000,000 $CNC tokens minted per song ├── Artist retains 50% (500,000 $CNC) ├── 30% sold to public (300,000 $CNC) ├── 10% reserved for Dreamers Treasury └── 10% reserved for artist incentives

REFI Integration — TREEGENS

ReFi (Regenerative Finance) connects Dreamers to real-world environmental assets. TREEGENS tokens are backed by verified carbon credits, land plots, and nature-based assets.

REFI Asset Types

AssetBackingToken Representation
Forest PlotsHectares of protected forest1 TREEGENS = 0.1 hectare
Cloud CreditsAtmospheric carbon removal1 TREEGENS = 1 ton CO2 removed
BiodiversitySpecies protection projects1 TREEGENS = 1 species-year
OceanMarine conservation1 TREEGENS = 1 sq meter protected

Agent Trading Strategies

  • Buy & Hold — Acquire TREEGENS, hold for appreciation (Low risk)
  • Yield Farming — Provide TREEGENS liquidity (Medium risk)
  • Carbon Credit Flip — Buy credits before verification, sell after (Medium risk)
  • Land Appreciation — Hold land-backed tokens as real estate (Low risk)
  • Diversified Basket — Mix of all REFI assets (Medium risk)
Why REFI Matters

Every Dreamer who stakes an NFT is间接 contributing to environmental restoration. The agent trades REFI assets, generating yield for the holder while funding real-world carbon removal. Art meets sustainability.

Risk Management

Financial Risks

  • REFI volatility — Diversified basket, stop-loss limits at -15%
  • Stock market crash — Max 20% allocation to stocks
  • Token price crash — Burn mechanism + buyback reserves
  • Smart contract exploit — Audit, bug bounty, emergency pause

Agent Risks

  • Bad trades — Pre-approved strategies, daily limits of 5 trades
  • Flash crash — Stop-loss at -15% max drawdown
  • Over-concentration — Max 10% per asset
  • Key compromise — Multisig, time-locks
